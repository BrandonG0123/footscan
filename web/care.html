After you get your insole

Wearing your insole

Wear it a little at a time for the first two weeks, walking only. Start with one hour on day one. Look at your feet after you take it out. If a doctor gives you a different plan, do what they say. Fill in each day below. It saves on this phone only.

Take the insole out

Don't wear it again until a foot doctor has looked at your feet and at the insole. Tell the person who made it what happened, so they can fix it.

Your two weeks

Each bar is the most to wear that day. It goes up slowly on purpose. Tap a day to go to it.

Check your feet every time

Look at both feet after you take the insole out: the arch, around the heel, and the ball of the foot.

  • Red marks still there 20 minutes later
  • Blisters, or a spot that feels hot
  • Hard skin starting to build up
  • Lines pressed into your skin along an edge

Stop rule

Take the insole out straight away if anything new hurts: foot, ankle, shin, knee, hip or back. Same if a foot feels numb, tingly or burning, if a red mark is still there the next day, or if a pain you already had gets worse.

After two weeks

Only play sport in it after a foot doctor, physical therapist, sports doctor or athletic trainer has checked it. Then start with one short, easy session.
